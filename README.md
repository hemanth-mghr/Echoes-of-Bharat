# Echoes of Bharat
Echoes of Bharat is a 2D historical action-adventure developed in Godot, centred on Emperor Ashoka and his journey from conquest to the aftermath of the Kalinga War. It combines exploration, platforming, combat, strategic choices, and a Dynamic WorldState & Consequence System. 

<img width="1127" height="617" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d1283c74-84a5-4cb7-995b-5801b4414727" />


• Dynamic Consequences: Player actions affect trust, stability, resources, relationships, military support, and NPC behaviour, shaping future events. 
• Exploration & Interaction: Explore settlements, forests, cities, courts, and battlefields, while interacting with NPCs and the environment. 
• Action & Platforming: Navigate 2D environments, overcome obstacles, and engage in action-based combat. 
• Historical Narrative: Experience Ashoka’s rise, expansion, Kalinga War, and its aftermath, leading toward his transformation and Dhamma. 
• Indian Culture: Experience architecture, traditions, governance, trade, and regional identities through gameplay.
